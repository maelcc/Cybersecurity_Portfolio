The Protagonists

Geralt of Rivia

Geralt of Rivia is a Witcher, a Witcher is a mutant made for hunting and killing monsters. And he always travels with his horse that he calls 'Roach'. He was given away as a kid by his mother to train at the School of the Wolf at Kaer Morhen (it's a castle/stronghold located in the mountains). And his mentor (and father-figure) is called Vesimir, another Witcher who trained him to become one.

Interestingly, Geralt does not come from Rivia, he added that location to his name as he started to work as a Witcher. Every Witcher has to undergo what is called 'The Trial of the Grasses' of which only 3 out of 10 boys survive. This trial mutates the body to gain various traits such as resistance to injury and slow aging. Geralt had some unexpected external mutations which led to his pale skin and white hair, which is why he is also called 'The White Wolf'.

Cirilla Fiona Elen Riannon

Cirilla Fiona Elen Riannon (known as Ciri), is the only princess of Cintra. She and Geralt know each other primarily because Geralt has saved her father, and his reward was invoking the 'Law of Surprise'. This law dictates that a man is expected to offer to his saviour a favour whose nature is unknown to (usually) both. In most cases, the favour takes the form of the saved man's firstborn child.

She sees Geralt as a father figure as he sees her as a daughter figure, and she is essential to the adventures they go through primarily because she has what is called 'Elder Blood', which is invaluable to the enemies. This is also the only reason why she survived 'The Trial of The Grasses' and became the first female Witcher.

Yennefer of Vengeberg

Yennefer of Vengeberg (called 'Yen' by Geralt) is a sorceress who lived in Vengeberg. She was brought to Aretuza to study magic, and for more than 30 years after her ascension, she met Geralt.

To Geralt, she is his true love (under particular circumstances explained in the series, books and videogames). And she sees Ciri as her daughter to the extent that she does everything in her power to rescue her and keep her safe.

Julian Alfred Pankratz

Julian Alfred Pankratz (known as Jaskier (in the TV series) or Dandelion (in the videogames)), is a bard and a close friend of Geralt. He met Geralt at a local tavern and accompanied him in a contract to kill a 'devil' that was stealing grain. He accompanies him because he writes ballads to improve Geralt's image so that he was no longer seen as a monstruous killer, which is how all Witchers are seen by the people.

Not much is known about Dandelion's family except that he was of noble birth.
