Netflix TV Series

Overview

The Netflix series of The Witcher came out December 20th 2019 and it quickly attracted many people, notably those that have read the books and played the games. It was developed by Lauren Schmidt Hissrich and led by henry Cavill as the main protagonist Geral of Rivia. This series 'adapts' the books and stories written by Andrzej Sapkowski, which place Geralt in a world of monsters, magic, and political tension. These stories show the lives of the Witcher Geralt, a powerful sorceress called Yennefer, and a princess Cirilla. These stories are set in a world of ruined kingdoms by war and dark folklore. This show emphasises the costs of power and the burden of destiny, which are themes that repeat themselves throughout Geralt's journey.

Episode 1, Season 1 - 'The End's Beginning'

The first episode of the series sets the style of it through tone and visual style. It brings the viewers into Geralt's very solitary and morally conflicted life as a monster-hunter that hunts for coin. He fights fictional beasts whilst dealing with the hatred of his kind by the people. This episode shows some of the Witcher's dangerous adventures along with some scenes of Yennefer's tortured past and Ciri's early stages of her powers. The non-linear storytelling of all three characters hints the viewer that they are all destined to meet at some point. This episode highlights themes such as destiny/fate, loss, and transformation too.

Episode 2, Season 1 - 'Four Marks'

The second episode explores more personal and also political histories. It is focused more on Yennefer's past and journey to magic, showing her struggles due to being disfigured. On the other side, Geralt's journey leads him into a tense political environment where there is a lot of moral ambiguity. The story here begins to show broader conflicts across what is called 'the Continent' with more complex power plays among different kingdoms around the Continent.
