Summary

Origins and Expansions

The Witcher initially comes from a series of 9 books and 15 short stories written by the Polish author Andrzej Sapkowski. With the first book being released in 1990, and the latest book released in 2024.

It then grew into a videogame franchise, with 3 total games (released in 2007, 2011, and 2015), developed by CD Projekt Red (a fourth game is coming next year).

It also grew into a TV series in 2019, ditrected by Tomasz Bagiński and funded by Netflix, with currently 3 seasons (the fourth season is coming later this year).

Short Summary

The Witcher takes place in the Middle Ages of a different universe around the 1200s with a pretty big twist.

The stories are focused on the Witcher Geralt of Rivia, along with interesting characters and a whole lot of beasts.
