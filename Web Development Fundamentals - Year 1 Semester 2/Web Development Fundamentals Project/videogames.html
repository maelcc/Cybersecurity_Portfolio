Videogames

'The Witcher' - 2007

The first Witcher game simply called 'The Witcher' was released in 2007 by CD Projekt Red. It marked the beginning of the franchise's 'change' from books to videogames. This RPG (role-playing game) has introduced players to Geralt of Rivia through a story about both personal loss, redemption, and fighting beasts. The game's storylines and the player's decisions (with consequences) set a new standard in RPGs. Despite the technical limitations the game had, The Witcher was appreciated for its mature storytelling, atmosphere, and complex dialogue, which established the foundation for becoming a loved videogame franchise.

'The Witcher 2: Assassin of Kings' - 2011

The second game called 'The Witcher 2: Assassin of Kings' was released in 2011 and advanced the storytelling and graphics of the game's franchise all because of the success of the first game. This sequel stands out from the first with its political plot in which Geralt is involved in the murder of a king along with conspiracies and changing alliances among different factions. The game's story and decision-making increased the importance the player's role in the game, leading to multiple endings that varied based on the choices made throughout the story. With its sophisticated character development and morally complex quests, The Witcher 2 was critically acclaimed and solidified CD Projekt Red's reputation.

'The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt' - 2015

The third game, 'The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt' was released in 2015, and it is seen as one of the best role-playing games ever. It follows Geralt's quest to find Cirilla whilst dealing with the Wild Hunt, a sort of force that haunts the Continent. And it does so whilst staying incredibly rich in detail. This third game is set in an open-world, meaning the player can virtually go anywhere he wants. Also, there are many different characters that offer dangerous and/or personal side-quests, adding more narrative to the game. This game has earned many prizes such as several 'Game of the Year' awards thanks to its great storytelling and immersion into the world. Because of this, there are also expansions to the game called 'Hearts of Stone' and 'Blood and Wine', which expand the lore even more.
